Week 1: Baseline & Comfort

This first week is about learning which movements feel good, not pushing to exhaustion. Keep effort around 4–6/10 and finish most sets feeling like you could do a few more clean reps.

Stop or modify an exercise if it causes sharp joint pain, increasing swelling, or nerve symptoms that spread farther down the leg. Seek medical care promptly for new severe weakness, bowel/bladder changes, or saddle-area numbness.
